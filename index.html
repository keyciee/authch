Our Principles

Identity
engineered with
Swiss precision

Switzerland did not earn its reputation for reliability by accident. Over centuries, Swiss institutions built trust through consistency, discretion, and an absolute refusal to compromise. AuthCH inherits every one of those values.

I

Precision

Authentication events are logged, timed, and verified to the millisecond. There is no ambiguity in identity.

II

Neutrality

No political allegiances. No commercial data sharing. AuthCH operates independently, as Switzerland always has.

III

Permanence

Infrastructure built to last decades, not quarters. Your identity layer should outlast your product roadmap.

Verlässlich.

Reliable. The single German word that defines the Swiss character — and the AuthCH guarantee.

What We Build

The complete
identity stack

From login to lifecycle — AuthCH provides the full authentication infrastructure that modern Swiss enterprises and global companies demand.

01

Multi-Factor Authentication

Adaptive MFA combining biometrics, hardware tokens, and behavioural signals — calibrated to your organisation's risk profile.

02

Single Sign-On

One verified identity across every application in your enterprise. SAML, OAuth 2.0, and OpenID Connect — fully compliant with Swiss and EU standards.

03

Decentralised Identity

Self-sovereign identity using W3C DID standards. Users own their credentials. You verify them. Nobody else touches them.

04

Regulatory Compliance

Built for nDSG, GDPR, FINMA, and emerging EU digital identity regulations. Compliance is architecture, not an afterthought.

05

Swiss Data Residency

All identity data processed and stored within Swiss borders. Sovereign infrastructure, outside foreign jurisdiction.

06

Enterprise API

Clean, versioned, and documented RESTful APIs. Integrate AuthCH into any stack in hours, not months.
